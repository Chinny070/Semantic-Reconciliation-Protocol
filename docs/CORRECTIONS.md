# Correction lineage

Orders are immutable after creation; v1 does not implement order amendments. Registered deliveries and invoices are immutable. A correction names its predecessor and creates a successor pointer; a record with a successor is superseded and cannot be newly reconciled. `get_delivery_record` and `get_invoice_record` keep predecessor and successor records readable. A corrected invoice may retain its invoice number only when it names the existing, unsuperseded invoice as its predecessor; a second unrelated invoice with that number remains rejected.

Historical reconciliation certificates remain unchanged. Delivery and invoice successors can be registered only by the original registrar, and superseded records cannot be reconciled. A receipt starts provisional: a sustained evidence-backed challenge can release its provisional quantities only after checking every order and source-line ledger invariant, while a finalized receipt cannot be challenged. Corrected records are then reconciled as a new pair, preserving the original certificate and its challenge history. Orders remain immutable; no payment or escrow behavior is introduced.
