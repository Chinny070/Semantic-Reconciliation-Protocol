# Substitution policy

The order freezes `EXACT_REQUIRED`, `EQUIVALENT_ALLOWED`, `SUPERIOR_ALLOWED`, `NAMED_ALTERNATIVES_ONLY`, or `NO_SUBSTITUTION`. Consensus classifies the semantic relation. Deterministic policy logic decides whether the classified relation is admissible. A superior-sounding or similar substitute is not accepted unless the frozen policy permits it. Ambiguous and inferior findings do not consume quantity.
