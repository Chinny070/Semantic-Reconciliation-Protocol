## GenLayer fit

The irreducible judgment is whether different descriptions refer to the same product, service, substitution, or charge. Deterministic code cannot establish that reliably from prose. Removing GenLayer requires trusting one buyer, supplier, OCR vendor, or centralized matcher. Validators independently re-observe registered public evidence and classify critical edges; deterministic code alone decides quantities, ratios, ceilings, consumption and status.

## Three-consumer proof

1. Wholesale procurement: purchase order ↔ warehouse receipt ↔ supplier invoice.
2. Construction: bill of quantities ↔ site delivery/progress record ↔ contractor invoice.
3. Logistics: shipping order ↔ proof of delivery ↔ carrier invoice.

The same contract interface and certificate can serve all three without a frontend or accounting backend.

## Hardest risk and evidence strategy

The hardest risk is substantive validator equivalence under changing external evidence and model variance. Each run retrieves each registered web record once per node, derives typed findings, and compares all decision-critical fields. Missing or ambiguous evidence fails closed. Public fixtures provide repeatable live evidence; real-world records are not claimed. Live packaging classification varied across attempts and the latest prompt clarification was not verified on the latest deployment because the Studionet RPC became unavailable.

## Standalone Intelligent Contract rationale

The portable, versioned certificate and consumption ledger are the reusable primitive. Semantic uncertainty is load-bearing and independently observable. No application layer or money movement is necessary.
