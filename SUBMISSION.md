# Submission preparation

**Title:** Semantic Reconciliation Protocol — Consensus-backed order, delivery, and invoice matching

**Portal description:**

Semantic Reconciliation Protocol is a reusable GenLayer Intelligent Contract for reconciling immutable orders, delivery records, and invoices. Validators independently re-observe configured public evidence and classify bounded semantic line-item and semantic-obligation relationships. Deterministic contract logic enforces order-frozen unit conversions, quantity limits, typed commercial ceilings, substitution policy, duplicate-consumption prevention, immutable delivery/invoice successor lineage, and a portable reconciliation certificate. Agreed external-evidence failure produces a typed zero-consumption certificate. V1 does not amend sealed orders or reverse finalized consumption; corrections requiring those operations are outside v1. This contract-only primitive does not move money.
